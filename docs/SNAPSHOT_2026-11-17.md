## What the day found instead

1. **A fault of mine in the lives** (docs/CRAFT.md): every 50,000 moments a life reloaded the old world. Lives born
   in world 2 or 3 since 11-12 lived world 1 from then on. Fixed; checkpoints now record what was lived.
2. **The cortex's own development was the fault of the eyes.** Its developed first layer reads out worse than
   filters never developed (0.250 / 0.441 against 0.276 / 0.484, COCO things / CIFAR + Commons); deeper layers of
   its kind add nothing (a fourth: 0.273 against 0.278). A layer with decorrelated windows, a graded response,
   256 kinds and an averaged summary: 0.383 / 0.545 (the whole old cortex: 0.278 / 0.487). `organic/cortex2.py`.
3. **The word cells could not use a better code.** A linear readout on the very pictures they lived names 0.56;
   they named 0.40, and a better cortex alone gave them +0.016. A decorrelating relay between the cortex and the
   word cells, and corrections at full size, take them to **0.516 held-out naming (three seeds) from 0.387**.
4. **Full-size corrections help words heard as much as pictures seen**: text beyond the parrot 0.248 → 0.330 offline
   (three seeds).
5. **Pruning** (three seeds): half of the word cells' synapses do nothing; four in five can go for 0.01 of picture
   naming after living on. It makes the same knowledge smaller; it does not raise a level.
6. **Negatives**: dendritic branches against forgetting; graded ("soft") competition in my version; a simple
   centre-surround retina in place of whitening; a second layer (adds 0.01); teaching the cortex's second layer;
   more old-style layers; replay (earlier); looking with the old eyes.
7. **World 3, first 50,000 moments** (valid part): Commons naming up 0.06, CIFAR naming down 0.11, text +0.045,
   last-word habit lower; forgetting of COCO things once their lessons stop is erasure in the word cells.
